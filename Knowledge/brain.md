# Intellia Company Brain

## Company Context

Intellia is an autonomous B2B Revenue Intelligence and GTM Orchestration Platform. It unifies structured CRM data like accounts, contacts, and deals with unstructured signal streams like M&A events, executive movements, intent scores, emails, and meetings.

The platform helps revenue teams understand pipeline health, improve win rates, and act on real-time next-best-action recommendations.

## Core Promise

Intellia turns scattered GTM data into clear revenue actions.

Instead of asking sales, marketing, RevOps, and customer success teams to manually inspect CRM fields, meeting notes, email threads, and market signals, Intellia identifies what matters and recommends what to do next.

## Ideal Customer Profile

Best-fit companies:

- B2B SaaS, cloud, data, cybersecurity, fintech, healthcare technology, and enterprise software companies
- 100 to 5,000 employees
- Mid-market and enterprise sales motion
- Annual contract values above $25K
- Multiple GTM teams using CRM, sales engagement, marketing automation, and meeting tools
- Complex buying committees with long sales cycles
- Active expansion, renewal, or enterprise account management motion

Strong fit indicators:

- CRM data exists but is incomplete, stale, or hard to trust
- Leaders struggle to explain pipeline risk before the quarter ends
- Sales reps miss important account signals
- RevOps spends too much time building manual reports
- Marketing intent data is not connected to account action
- Customer success teams need earlier renewal and expansion signals

Poor fit indicators:

- Very small teams with simple founder-led sales
- Low ACV transactional sales
- Companies without a CRM or consistent account ownership
- Teams that only need static dashboards, not recommended actions

## Important Signals

High-priority buying signals:

- Intent score spike
- Funding round
- Acquisition or merger event
- New executive hire
- Champion promotion
- Expansion into new markets
- Increase in hiring for sales, RevOps, or data teams
- Multiple engaged contacts in one account
- Positive meeting or email sentiment

High-priority risk signals:

- Executive departure
- Champion leaves account
- Deal stage has not changed
- Negative email sentiment
- Missed meetings
- Procurement delay
- Budget concern
- Competitor mentioned
- Renewal date approaching without engagement

## Outreach Playbooks

### Intent Spike Playbook

Trigger: Account shows a high intent score around revenue intelligence, RevOps automation, forecasting, or GTM orchestration.

Goal: Start a timely conversation while the account is actively researching.

Recommended action:

- Send a personalized email referencing the likely business problem
- Route the account to the owner or SDR
- Add the account to a high-priority sequence
- Recommend a relevant case study or benchmark

Sample message:

Subject: Noticed your team may be exploring revenue intelligence

Hi {{first_name}},

Teams usually look at platforms like Intellia when pipeline visibility, account signals, or forecast risk are getting harder to manage manually.

Intellia connects CRM data with signals from meetings, emails, intent, and market events so revenue teams can see where to focus and what action to take next.

Worth a quick conversation?

### Champion Movement Playbook

Trigger: Known champion is promoted, changes role, or joins a target account.

Goal: Re-engage the champion and open a strategic conversation.

Recommended action:

- Send a congratulatory note
- Reference prior context if available
- Suggest a conversation about their new revenue priorities
- Alert the account owner

Sample message:

Subject: Congrats on the new role

Hi {{first_name}},

Congratulations on the new role. Given your focus on revenue operations and GTM execution, I thought it might be useful to reconnect.

Intellia helps teams unify CRM, buyer engagement, and account signals into real-time next-best actions for sales and RevOps teams.

Would it be useful to compare notes on your priorities for the new team?

### Executive Change Playbook

Trigger: CRO, VP Sales, CMO, CIO, or Customer Success executive joins or leaves a target account.

Goal: Map the new stakeholder and identify strategic initiatives.

Recommended action:

- Identify the new executive owner
- Review open opportunities and active contacts
- Send a short insight-led message
- Recommend executive-level outreach from the AE or sales leader

Sample message:

Subject: Revenue visibility during leadership transition

Hi {{first_name}},

Leadership changes often create a good moment to reassess pipeline visibility, account prioritization, and GTM operating rhythm.

Intellia helps revenue teams connect CRM data with real-time account signals so leaders can quickly see pipeline risk and recommended next actions.

Open to a brief conversation?

### M&A Event Playbook

Trigger: Target account announces acquisition, merger, or major restructuring.

Goal: Position Intellia as the operating layer for complex GTM change.

Recommended action:

- Identify likely GTM integration pain points
- Prioritize enterprise stakeholders
- Recommend messaging around account consolidation and pipeline visibility
- Create an executive outreach task

Sample message:

Subject: GTM visibility after the acquisition

Hi {{first_name}},

After an acquisition, revenue teams often need a clearer way to understand overlapping accounts, pipeline risk, stakeholder changes, and expansion opportunities.

Intellia helps unify CRM data with external and engagement signals so GTM teams can prioritize the right accounts and actions.

Would it be worth sharing how teams use this during GTM integration?

### Stalled Deal Playbook

Trigger: Opportunity has not progressed, engagement is declining, or close date risk is increasing.

Goal: Help the rep unblock the opportunity.

Recommended action:

- Review recent emails and meetings
- Check champion strength
- Identify missing stakeholders
- Recommend a value recap, executive alignment call, or mutual action plan

Sample message:

Subject: Checking in on next steps

Hi {{first_name}},

I wanted to reconnect on the Intellia conversation. Based on where teams usually get value, the next useful step is often aligning on the revenue workflows where better account signals and next-best-action recommendations would have the biggest impact.

Should we schedule a short working session with the right stakeholders?

## Common Objections

### We already have dashboards.

Dashboards show what happened. Intellia recommends what to do next by connecting CRM data with live GTM signals.

### Our CRM already tracks opportunities.

CRM fields are useful, but they often miss buyer sentiment, external account changes, champion movement, and real-time intent. Intellia adds the signal layer around CRM.

### We already use intent data.

Intent data is most valuable when it is tied to account ownership, deal context, and recommended action. Intellia turns intent into GTM execution.

### Our reps already know their accounts.

Great reps still miss signals across emails, meetings, executive changes, and market events. Intellia helps them focus faster and act with better context.
